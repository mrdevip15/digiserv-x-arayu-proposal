# Arayu Aesthetic Clinic — Strategic Digital Proposal 2026

A comprehensive digital transformation proposal by **Digiserv.ID** for **Arayu Aesthetic Clinic**. This project outlines a strategic roadmap to elevate the clinic's digital presence, optimize operational efficiency, and enhance the patient experience through intelligent automation and personalized data.

## 🚀 Overview

This repository contains the strategic proposal and deep analysis for Arayu Aesthetic Clinic's digital transformation in 2026. It focuses on solving key pain points identified from patient feedback and clinical operational needs.

## 📂 Project Structure

-   **[index.html](index.html)**: The main strategic digital proposal. An interactive, premium landing page designed for stakeholders.
-   **[arayu-deep-analysis.html](arayu-deep-analysis.html)**: A deep dive into patient complaints based on Google Maps reviews, matched with specific digital solutions.
-   **[demo-mvp/](demo-mvp/)**: A showcase of the proposed Minimum Viable Product (MVP) interfaces, including:
    -   `home.png`: Main landing page interface.
    -   `booking.png`: Intelligent reservation system.
    -   `portal.png`: Exclusive patient portal.
    -   `queue.png`: Digital queue management.
    -   `admin.png`: Operational control center.

## 💡 Key Strategies

1.  **Data Integration**: Unifying patient records to eliminate fragmentation.
2.  **Smart Automation**: Implementing CRM and automated follow-ups.
3.  **Premium Patient Experience**: Introducing a Patient Progress Portal and loyalty systems.
4.  **Operational Efficiency**: Digital queue management and transparent pricing.
